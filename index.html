About the Alliance

Building community, one storefront at a time.

The Uptown Lakewood Business Alliance has connected the west end of Madison Avenue since 2007. We're a coalition of independent business owners working to make this stretch of street the most vibrant, welcoming corridor in Lakewood.

Connect Businesses

Networking, shared promotion, and collective advocacy for the district's independent operators.

Guide Visitors

Helping people discover the west end: where to eat, shop, explore, and spend an afternoon.

Engage Community

Events, initiatives, and partnerships that make the neighborhood feel like home.

Help New Business

Resources and support for entrepreneurs ready to open on Madison Avenue.

Halloween on Madison

Uptown Lakewood's
Trick or Treat

Costumes on, bags ready. Once a year the shops on Madison Avenue open their doors to the neighborhood's ghosts, goblins, and superheroes for an evening of treats on the west end.

  • When 5:00 – 7:30 pmRain date: Wednesday, October 21
  • Where Madison AvenueUptown Lakewood, west end
  • Cost FreeAll ages welcome

To keep traffic moving, pick up and drop off at one of two spots: Reveley at Madison (by Walgreens) or Morrison at Madison.

Please walk with the flow of traffic to avoid congestion on the sidewalks.
